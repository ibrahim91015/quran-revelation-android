## What this release adds

The new **Revelation** tab lists all 114 surahs in [Tanzil’s traditional revelation order](https://tanzil.net/docs/revelation_order). Audio started from that tab follows the same sequence: **Al-‘Alaq (96) → Al-Qalam (68) → Al-Muzzammil (73)**, continuing through **An-Nasr (110)** and then stopping.

The Quran text, canonical surah numbers, normal verse order, and mushaf pages are preserved. The sequence is at the surah level, based on each surah’s first revealed verses. Sessions started from the normal Surahs tab keep canonical order.

### Reciters and audio

- Retains all **89 recitation entries** from the supplied official Quran.com 3.6.4 APK, including **70 gapless entries** and **44 Opus alternatives**.
- Preserves reciter IDs, translated name resources, MP3/Opus URLs, storage paths, and timing-database mappings.
- Prefers downloaded files and streams missing audio as needed, including at revelation-order surah boundaries.
- Supports the existing verse/range repeats, playback speed, previous/next verse controls, pause/resume, and background playback in the selected queue order.
- Handles RTL tab order and translated surah-name preferences.

## Known limitations

- Device validation used an Android 16 x86_64 emulator. Physical-phone and other device/version combinations remain to be tested.
- Gapless playback is continuous within a surah’s audio file. Streaming a new surah can involve buffering; availability also depends on external audio hosts.
- Quran.com account sync needs independently registered OAuth configuration for this app. Local reading, bookmarks, downloads, and audio work without sign-in.
- The APK’s supported Android range is broader than the device configurations exercised by the playback tests.
